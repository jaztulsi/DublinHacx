OUR MISSION

Bring an idea, get 12 hours to build it, and show what you made by the end of the day. No themes, no required tracks — just a room full of builders, free food, mentors, and the clock.

WHY WE'RE UNIQUE

Dublin Hacx is Dublin's very first Dublin Hacx — a single-day, 12-hour hackathon built by students, for students. It's run out of the Emerald Hacking & Coding Club at Emerald High School, not a company or a university. That means every dollar sponsors put in goes straight to meals, swag, and prizes for 170 hackers, and every sponsor gets a direct line to the students actually building. There are no themes and no boxes to fit inside — just a working demo by 8pm.

Questions,
answered.

Who can attend?
Any high school student in the Bay Area. You don’t need to be from Dublin or Emerald HS — bring your friends from any school.
Why should I sponsor Dublin Hacx?
You get in front of 170 of the Bay Area’s most driven high-school builders for a full 12 hours, with opt-in access to their resumes, GitHubs, and projects — and your brand ends up in every photo and every story they tell afterward.
How can I help?
Beyond a check, we welcome mentors, judges, and workshop leaders. You can also donate hardware, API or cloud credits, food, swag, or prizes — recognition is matched to what you contribute.
Where will my money go?
100% of funds go straight to meals, swag, and prizes for 170 students to build for free. Dublin Hacx is run through the Emerald Hacking & Coding Club — by students, for students.
